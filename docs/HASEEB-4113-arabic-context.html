Legend

  • Key — the path in src/copy/ar.json. Array items are shown as name[0], name[1] …
  • English source — the same key in en.json, with every {placeholder} replaced by the value the build actually renders from src/fixture/sample-company.json. The copy files never contain a figure.
  • Current Arabic — exactly what ar.json holds today. awaiting review marks the 8 strings round 7 deliberately left in English (founder §3 and §5: no new Arabic was authored this round); the Arabic page shows the English sentence, printed underneath. same as EN — by design marks the 6 strings that are identical because there is nothing to translate — a Latin wordmark, a mailbox address, a merchant name as it prints on a statement.
  • Where it appears — page, section, element and, where it matters, the state and the moment in time.
  • Screenshot — a crop of the live Arabic page at localhost:4113/ar with that string's own element outlined in a 2 px teal box. One image per string, so the outline always points at the string in its row; click it for the full-size PNG. Desktop crops are 1440 px wide; the phone-only rows (the burger label, the sticky bar) are 390 px. The opening film was frozen with its own clock and captured at 1.2 s (the drifting field) and 5.4 s (the three-line block).
  • Length limitation — measured in the browser, not estimated: the element's available content width at 1440 and 390, how many lines the current text takes, and any hard single-line constraint.

Before reading the rows

  • Growth is mostly free, height is not. Almost every element here wraps. The ones that cannot are the three status pills, the amount cells, the launcher's teal tag and the two form inputs — those are called out in the last column. Everywhere else, a longer Arabic line pushes the block taller rather than clipping.
  • The film's word list is sampled, and the sample is fixed. The 34 tokens on a 1440 field are drawn from the 19-entry vocabulary by a seeded generator, so the same entries appear on every run. Five of the nineteen — سداد · توصيل · إيصال · تحويل بنكي and Invoice — are never drawn at 1440. They are still worth reviewing (the sample changes the moment the vocabulary does), but they are not on screen today.
  • Two full film frames, for the moments the crops are cut from: the drifting field at 1.2 s and the three-line block at 5.4 s.
  • The Arabic film vocabulary is one entry shorter than the English. ar.json has 19 tokens, en.json has 20; the English list ends with an Arabic garnish token (د.ك {row1Amount}) that the Arabic list does not mirror. Nothing breaks — the build only needs at least one word and one amount — but the two lists are not a key-for-key pair.
